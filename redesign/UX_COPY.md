# NotAI — UX copy guide (pt-BR + en)

## Voice
Calm, concrete, adult. The app is a tool for money, so it earns trust by being plain. Short sentences. Numbers do the talking.

**Never:** emoji, exclamation marks (one allowed per flow at genuine success), hype ("inteligência espacial", "neural", "supercharge"), fake chattiness ("E aí!", "Show!"), robot-persona theatre ("Estou ouvindo...", "Sistemas on-line"), gamer slang.
**Always:** sentence case (no ALL-CAPS labels), verbs for buttons, specifics over adjectives ("18% acima do mês passado", not "gastos altos!").

## Neo repositioning
Neo is no longer a character that talks. The word "Neo" disappears from the UI. AI output is labeled **Insight**, always signed with its source: *"Gerado a partir dos seus lançamentos"* / *"Generated from your entries"*. Insights state a fact, then at most one suggestion. No first person.

- Before: "E aí! Eu sou o Neo, seu parceiro de inteligência financeira."
- After: "Mercado está 18% acima do mês passado. Média das últimas 4 semanas: R$ 96/semana."

## Errors
State what happened + what to do. Never blame, never joke.
- pt: "Não conseguimos ler este cupom. Tente com mais luz, ou lance manualmente." / en: "We couldn't read this receipt. Try better lighting, or enter it manually."
- pt: "Sem conexão. Seus lançamentos ficam salvos no aparelho." / en: "No connection. Your entries are saved on this device."
